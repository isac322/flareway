The chart bundles only Flareway CRDs. Helm retains CRDs and the system namespace on uninstall.
Uninstalling the chart therefore does not delete Flareway custom resources or trigger their remote deletion policies.
No uninstall hook contacts Cloudflare or deletes remote state.
